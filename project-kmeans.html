Which Ontario cities needed help first?

My undergraduate research project grouped 34 Ontario cities by three counts: resolved, not resolved and fatal cases. The most useful thing I did was remove one city.

10 tiles = 1 city. Groups are from K-means with three clusters. The last row of pale tiles is spare.

One outlier was hiding the next hot spots

With Toronto in the data, K-means gave it a group of its own and lumped Mississauga and Newmarket in with eleven other cities. Remove Toronto, and those two stand apart as the next highest.

Switch between runs to see who moves

Groups as listed in my report, highest case counts first.

The two methods disagreed

Hierarchical clustering put 30 cities in one group, mixing places with high and low counts, so I judged it less accurate here. It did single out Ottawa, which K-means left in the middle group. I named four places for the tightest measures: Toronto, Mississauga, Newmarket and Ottawa.

I chose three clusters using the elbow and silhouette methods. With Toronto removed and four clusters allowed, both methods gave identical groups.

What I'd do differently

These are total counts, not rates. Bigger cities record more cases, so part of what the clusters measure is population. I'd redo this per 100,000 people before anyone based a policy on it.
